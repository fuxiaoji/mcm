## 📈 Figure 1: 核心结果汇总 (6合1)

**文件**: `Q1_Fig1_KeyResults_6in1.png` (300 DPI)

**尺寸**: 18×12 英寸 (适合全页展示或缩放为半页)

### 子图说明

| 面板 | 标题 | 内容描述 | 关键发现 |
|-----|------|---------|---------|
| (A) | Distribution of Certainty | 确定性指数分布直方图 | 平均确定性 ~0.89，大多数估计高度可靠 |
| (B) | Certainty by Season | 各赛季确定性箱线图 | 跨34个赛季确定性稳定，无明显趋势 |
| (C) | Consistency by Group Size | 按参与人数分组的一致性率 | 小组(2-5人)一致性较低 |
| (D) | Judge Score Dispersion | 评委分数变异系数分布 | 大多数事件CV在0.05-0.15之间 |
| (E) | Dispersion vs Certainty | 离散度与确定性散点图 | 正相关(r值显示在图中) |
| (F) | Participants vs Certainty | 参与人数与确定性关系 | 人数越多，确定性越高 |

### 论文引用示例

> Figure X presents the key results of the fan vote reconstruction model. Panel (A) shows that the mean certainty index is approximately 0.89, indicating high model reliability. Panel (B) demonstrates consistent performance across all 34 seasons. The positive correlation between judge score dispersion and model certainty (Panel E) suggests that greater score variance provides more informative constraints for vote reconstruction.
