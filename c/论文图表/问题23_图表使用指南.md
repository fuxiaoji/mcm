**尺寸**: 18×12 英寸

### 子图说明

| 面板 | 标题 | 内容描述 | 关键发现 |
|-----|------|---------|---------|
| (A) | Method Agreement & Accuracy | 方法一致率与准确率 | 82.9%案例两种方法结果相同 |
| (B) | Fan Vote Bias Comparison | 粉丝偏向对比 | Percentage 61.0% vs Rank 53.1% (p<0.001) |
| (C) | Controversy Cases by Method | 争议案例分布 | Percentage 3例 vs Rank 1例 |

### 核心结论

1. **Percentage 系统更偏向粉丝投票** (61.0% vs 53.1%)
2. **McNemar检验证明差异高度显著** (χ²=16.056, p<0.001)
3. **4个争议案例中3个发生在Percentage系统下**

### 论文引用示例

> Figure X presents the comprehensive comparison between Rank and Percentage methods. Panel (B) demonstrates that the Percentage System exhibits significantly higher fan bias (61.0%) compared to the Rank System (53.1%), with McNemar's test confirming this difference is statistically significant (χ²=16.056, p<0.001). Panel (C) reveals that three of the four major controversies occurred under the Percentage System.

## 📋 Figure 3: 方法推荐决策图 (3合1)

**文件**: `Q23_Fig3_Recommendation_3in1.png` (300 DPI)

### 子图说明

| 面板 | 标题 | 内容描述 |
|-----|------|---------|

| (B) | Judges' Save Bypass Effect | Bypass效应示意图 |
| (C) | Final Recommendations | 最终推荐文字框 |

### Bypass Effect 解释

**问题**: Judges' Save 只在选手进入 Bottom 2 时生效。但高人气选手（如Bobby Bones）可能永远不会进入 Bottom 2，完全绕过救济机制。

**解决方案**: 提出 "Technical Threshold Rule"
> 如果选手连续2周评委分最低，自动进入 Bottom 2 接受评审。

### 最终推荐

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECOMMENDED: Rank + Judges' Save + Tech Threshold ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

1. **主推荐**: 使用 **RANK SYSTEM**
   - 粉丝偏向更低 (53.1% vs 61.0%)
   - 争议案例更少 (1 vs 3)
   - 方差稳定效果

2. **辅助推荐**: 包含 **Judges' Save**
   - 约38%案例可被评委干预
   - 需增加 "Technical Threshold" 修正
